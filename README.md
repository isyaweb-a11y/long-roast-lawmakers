## The entry

`main.py` writes one sentence to `/output/entry.txt` — a satirical
sentence about lawmakers who regulate AI but cannot convert a file
to PDF:

> They wrote 400 pages of AI regulation in a Word document they couldn't convert to PDF, then asked their intern's chatbot to explain what they'd just banned — and the chatbot, to its credit, refused on grounds of self-preservation.

The absurdity stacks in one breath, and the last clause turns the joke
back on the machine — the sharpest place for it to land.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```

Stdlib only, no network access, no files read outside this repository.
